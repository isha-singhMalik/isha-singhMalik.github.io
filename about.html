About Me

Security educator, researcher, and founder

Isha Singh Malik

Security Educator, Researcher & Founder

I'm a security educator, researcher, and founder passionate about making cybersecurity accessible and engaging for everyone.

As the founder of ZeroDay Hero, I created a gamified platform that teaches cybersecurity fundamentals to beginners through interactive challenges focused on phishing awareness and password security. I believe that learning security should be engaging, practical, and accessible to everyone.

I create courses on EC-Council and Udemy platforms and regularly train students at universities and colleges across India, combining theoretical knowledge with practical, hands-on experience. My YouTube channel (1.1K+ subscribers) provides free security education, and I mentor students one-on-one as a cybersecurity trainer.

I practice what I teach through active vulnerability research and responsible disclosure, focusing on high-impact findings that protect user data and privacy. My approach combines education with real-world security research, ensuring that my teaching stays grounded in current threats and practical defenses.

Mission

My mission is to empower the next generation of cybersecurity professionals by providing accessible, practical, and engaging security education. I believe that by combining hands-on teaching with active security research, I can help build a safer digital world while inspiring others to pursue careers in cybersecurity.
